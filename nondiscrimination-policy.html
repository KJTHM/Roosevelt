Roosevelt Academy Nondiscrimination Policy

Roosevelt Academy admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school.

Roosevelt Academy does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national or ethnic origin in the administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and financial aid programs, athletic programs, or other school-administered programs.

Roosevelt Academy

A nonprofit day school in Austin, Texas for students in grades 8 - 12 with disabilities and diverse learning needs.
